## Overview

Two to four sentences. What it is, who it's for, the context (competition, venue,
volunteer, coursework), and the outcome in one line.

## The problem

Why this mattered — one or two paragraphs. A couple of grounding numbers at most.
What existing approaches get wrong, in a sentence.

## What I did

Your ownership on the project: what you framed, built, and drove. One paragraph.
Delete this section for solo work.

## Building it

The technical core — two to four paragraphs or a short bullet list. Architecture,
the key decision(s), the piece you're proudest of and why. Use `###` for
sub-sections if it needs them.

## Outcome

What happened: result, publication, pitch, ship. One or two paragraphs. Include
honest context (what fell short) if it's part of the story.

## What I learned

One paragraph. Concrete, not generic.
